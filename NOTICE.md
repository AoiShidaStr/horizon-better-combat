# Notices

## This mod

`better_combat` for Horizon: original code, MIT (`LICENSE`), written for the private
"Saison 2" server. No file of another mod is copied or translated into this bundle.

## Upstream mods

This is an unaffiliated reimplementation of the *idea* of two NeoForge mods:

- **Better Combat** by daedelus — weapon-specific combos and wide swings.
- **Combat Roll** by wefcd — a dodge roll on a key.

Both are published All-Rights-Reserved. Their code, data files, models, textures, sounds and
lang files are **not** used here, in any form. The combos, the weapon profiles, the roll, the
message format and the HUD were written from the public description of what those mods do, and
every tuning number in `api/src/weapons.rs` and `api/src/core.rs` is this port's own.

## Horizon

The bundle links against `horizon-client`'s public mod API and is built by the Horizon launcher
against a pinned game commit. Horizon is licensed by its own authors; nothing of it is
redistributed here.
